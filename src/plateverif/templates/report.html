How to read this report

The report compares the house-number plates seen in the street photos with the official Paris address register.

Two points of view

Addresses (circles) start from the register: is the expected plate visible where it should be? This is the only way to find a missing plate.

Plates (diamonds) start from the photos: where is this plate, and is its number right for that spot? This is how wrong, misplaced and unknown numbers are found.

How a plate is placed on the map

Each photo gives a sight line from the camera towards the plate (dashed lines). When the same plate is seen in two or more photos, the lines cross at its position. With one photo only, the position is estimated from the distance to the facade, which is less reliable.

Confidence

Confidence says how strong the evidence is: number of photos, agreement of the readings and geometry. It does not change the result. Low-confidence results should be checked first.
