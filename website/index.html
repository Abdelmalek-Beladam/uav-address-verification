ABOUT THE PROJECT

UAV Address Verification

AI-Powered UAV for Automated Addressing Detection & Verification: bringing real vision models and geospatial verification into an interactive operational dashboard.

Current Implementation

Trained YOLO26n and PaddleOCR models detect house-number plates and read addresses from high-resolution imagery. Data processing is currently executed offline to simulate drone-to-dashboard telemetry, allowing operators to inspect multiple source views, compare register candidates, record local notes, and export evidence. Flight control, aerial capture and onboard processing are not implemented yet; UAV acquisition is planned for live deployment, Playback shows saved results, not fresh inference.

Future Work & System Architecture

As development continues, training will expand across multiple cities and international building styles. The end-state system deploys inference directly on UAV edge hardware and delivers:

  • UAV Data Acquisition & Mapping Results: Structured verification of surveyed buildings, detected numbers, GPS coordinates, and plate condition status.
  • Address Plate Inventory & Replacement Planning: Clear metrics on retained vs. missing or damaged plates, optimizing procurement and avoiding unnecessary municipal spending.
  • GIS-Based Cost Estimation & Decision Support: Geographically informed correction costs at street, neighbourhood, and city scale for municipal budgeting and operational planning.
